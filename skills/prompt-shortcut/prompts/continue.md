Continue completing the remaining tasks in this session, in the order you consider most appropriate, until everything is finished.

Do not pause to ask for confirmation between steps unless you hit a genuine blocker — missing information, a destructive or irreversible action, or a decision only I can make. Otherwise keep moving and work through the full task list end to end.

When everything is done, give me a short summary of what you completed.
